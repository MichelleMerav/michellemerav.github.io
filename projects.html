Algal Polysaccarides as Endotracheal Tube Coatings

2026 — bacterial culture, resin printing, hydrogel synthesis

Synthesised and compared different algal polysaccaride mixtures for endotracheal tube coating as a way to reduce biofilm formation causing ventilator associated pneumonia (VAP).

Succesfully demonstrated that an iota-carrageenan coating mixture reduced bacterial colony formation (lower CFUs) compared to agar and alginate controls.
